Creatine for College Students: A Simple Beginner Guide

Published September 12, 2026

Creatine monohydrate is one of the most commonly studied sports supplements. It is often used by lifters who want to support repeated high-intensity exercise and strength training.

Keep it simple

If you are considering creatine, use a reputable product, follow the label, stay normally hydrated, and talk with a qualified health professional if you have medical conditions or medication concerns.

Do not expect magic

Creatine does not replace training, sleep, calories, or protein. Those basics still matter much more than any supplement.
