Do Beginners Need Lifting Straps?

Published 2026-10-15

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Most beginners do not need a large collection of lifting accessories. Lifting straps can be useful when your grip becomes the limiting factor on certain pulling exercises, but they are not required to start making progress in the gym.

What do lifting straps do?

Lifting straps wrap around your wrist and the bar to help you hold onto the weight. This can make it easier to continue a pulling exercise when your back or other target muscles can handle more weight than your grip can.

When can straps be useful?

Straps can be helpful on exercises such as Romanian deadlifts, heavy rows, shrugs, or other pulling movements where your grip gives out before the muscles you are trying to train.

Do beginners need them?

Usually, no. If you are new to lifting, focus first on learning good technique and gradually getting stronger. Your grip will also have an opportunity to improve as you train.

Do not use them for everything

Even if you eventually buy straps, you do not have to use them on every pulling exercise. Training some sets without straps can still challenge your grip, while straps can be saved for sets where grip is preventing you from properly training the target muscle.

Are cheap straps enough?

You generally do not need an expensive pair just to try lifting straps. A simple, comfortable pair can be enough for a beginner. Pay attention to durability and how securely they fit rather than buying something just because it looks advanced.

Focus on the basics first

Straps are a tool, not a shortcut to getting stronger. A consistent training program, good technique, enough food, and recovery will matter much more for your progress than any lifting accessory.
