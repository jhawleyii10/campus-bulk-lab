12 Cheap Bulking Foods for College Students

Published September 12, 2026

Bulking does not have to mean buying expensive steaks, shakes, and supplements. The easiest approach is usually to build meals around foods that are cheap, calorie-dense, and easy to repeat.

Good budget staples

  • Eggs
  • Whole milk
  • Peanut butter
  • Rice
  • Oats
  • Ground beef or turkey
  • Chicken thighs
  • Greek yogurt
  • Bananas
  • Bagels
  • Cheese
  • Beans

Make the plan easy to repeat

Instead of trying to eat perfectly, pick a few breakfasts, lunches, and dinners you can repeat. Add calories with simple extras such as milk, peanut butter, olive oil, cheese, or an extra serving of rice.

Individual calorie and protein needs vary, so adjust portions based on your goals, activity, and professional guidance when needed.
