How to Build a High-Protein Dining Hall Plate

Published September 12, 2026

A dining hall can actually make bulking easier because you have multiple foods available in one place. The trick is building a plate with a repeatable structure.

The basic plate

  • Start with a clear protein source.
  • Add a carb such as rice, potatoes, pasta, bread, or fruit.
  • Add a calorie booster if you need more energy.
  • Include vegetables or fruit for variety and micronutrients.

If your first plate is small, get a second plate instead of trying to force everything into one giant meal.
