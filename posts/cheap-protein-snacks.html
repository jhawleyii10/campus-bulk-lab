10 Cheap High-Protein Snacks for College Students

Published 2026-09-21

Affiliate disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

Protein snacks do not have to be expensive or complicated. For college students, the best options are easy to keep in a dorm, dining hall, backpack, or mini fridge and can help you reach your daily protein goal between meals.

1. Greek yogurt

Greek yogurt is an easy high-protein snack that works well between classes. Add granola, fruit, or peanut butter when you also want more calories.

2. Milk

Milk is an inexpensive way to add protein and calories without another full meal. It can also be convenient with breakfast or after a workout.

3. Cottage cheese

Cottage cheese provides plenty of protein and can be eaten by itself or paired with fruit, crackers, or another simple snack.

4. Tuna packets

Tuna packets are portable and require almost no preparation. Pair one with crackers or bread for an easy snack with protein and carbohydrates.

5. String cheese

String cheese is simple to grab from a dining hall or keep refrigerated in a dorm. It is especially useful when you only need a small snack.

6. Hard-boiled eggs

Hard-boiled eggs are another simple option if you have access to them in your dining hall or can prepare and refrigerate them safely.

7. Peanut butter

Peanut butter is more calorie-dense than many other options and contains some protein. Pair it with bread, a bagel, banana, or crackers when you are trying to add calories.

8. Roasted edamame

Roasted edamame can provide protein in a shelf-stable snack that is easy to carry in a backpack.

9. Protein shake

A protein shake can be convenient when normal food is not practical. It should supplement regular meals rather than replace most of the food in your diet.

10. Leftovers from a meal

A snack does not have to come in a package. Leftover chicken, meat, rice, or another meal can be an inexpensive way to get more protein while avoiding wasted food.

Keep snacks simple

You do not need all ten options. Pick a few that fit your budget, schedule, and access to refrigeration. Consistently eating enough across your normal meals and snacks matters more than finding a perfect high-protein food.
